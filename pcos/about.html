Methodology & Privacy

Our Privacy Commitment

Your data never leaves your device.

This application runs entirely in your web browser. When you fill out the assessment, the calculations are performed locally on your computer or phone. We do not have a database, and we do not transmit, track, or store your health information.

How the Model Works

Our risk prediction is powered by a Logistic Regression machine learning model trained on a clinical dataset of 541 patients.

Feature Selection

To make this tool accessible without requiring blood tests or ultrasounds, the model was specifically trained using only 15 user-answerable features (such as age, BMI, menstrual cycle history, and visible symptoms). This allows for a preliminary screening but means the tool is less accurate than a full clinical diagnosis.

Risk Thresholds

The model outputs a probability score from 0 to 1, which we categorize into three bands based on statistical distributions in the training data:

  • Low Risk: Probability falls below the typical range seen in PCOS patients.
  • Moderate Risk: Probability is elevated and overlaps with both groups.
  • High Risk: Probability strongly aligns with patterns seen in PCOS patients.

Limitations

This tool is intended for educational and awareness purposes only. It cannot replace a clinical diagnosis.

Medical professionals diagnose PCOS based on the Rotterdam Criteria, which requires two of the following three: irregular periods, excess androgens (via blood test or symptoms), and polycystic ovaries (via ultrasound). Because our tool cannot perform lab tests or imaging, its results are purely estimates based on statistical correlation.
